# bright (the default)

**When:**
- science, health, food and everyday objects
- "how did people do X before Y"
- any topic that cuts between today and the past
- the safest choice when you are unsure

**STYLE** (paste this after `look: bright`):

hand-drawn 2D cartoon illustration for a history explainer channel, clean dark ink outlines, flat colours with soft shading, a bright natural and varied colour palette like a modern flat cartoon: clear blue skies, fresh greens, clean whites, light pastel walls, colourful objects, balanced neutral daylight. Not sepia, not brown, not golden or orange colour cast. Full-bleed background that fills the whole frame, simple and readable, 16:9. People are simple round-head stick-figure characters with dot eyes and thin limbs.

**Character sheet** (text-to-image, one generation with `--outputs 2`; keep the better one):

Character design sheet for a 2D cartoon explainer: <N> simple stick-figure style characters, each shown front view, three-quarter view and side view, same scale, on a plain light-grey backdrop. All have a big perfectly round head with plain light-beige skin, two small black dot eyes, a simple curved line mouth, a thin neck, and thin stick-like arms and legs with small round hands. <For each one, left to right: 'name': hair, clothes with colours, one prop.> Clean dark ink outlines, flat colours with soft shading, friendly and simple. No text, no letters, no labels, no watermark.

**Notes:**
- Close-ups of food and objects pick up random background people unless the prompt says
  "no people".
- Maps: write "simple bright cartoon map of <region>, green land and blue sea, no text, no
  borders, a red location pin on <place>". Rough maps are fine.
