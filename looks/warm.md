# warm

**When:** ancient humans, early civilisations, religion and myth, villages, Indian
history, and Hindi or Hinglish channels. This is the look of the Hindi videos that passed
a million views.

**STYLE** (paste this after `look: warm`):

warm hand-drawn 2D cartoon illustration for a history explainer channel, thick clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat warm colours with soft painterly shading and subtle paper grain, golden ochre and amber dominant palette, a full-bleed colourful background that fills the entire frame (never a white or blank background), cinematic warm light, simple and readable, 16:9. People are simple round-head stick-figure characters with dot eyes and thin limbs.

**Character sheet:** use the same prompt as `bright`, with two changes:
- the backdrop is "a warm golden-ochre textured paper backdrop with a soft vignette"
- the drawing style is "thick clean dark-brown ink outlines, flat warm colours with soft
  painterly shading"

**Notes:**
- A whole film in this look turns brown-orange. A tester liked it but found it "too
  brownish".
  - Break it up where the story allows: blue night skies, green forests, white moments.
  - Write those colours into the prompts of those shots.
- Food close-ups pick up white-headed background cooks unless the prompt says "no people".
